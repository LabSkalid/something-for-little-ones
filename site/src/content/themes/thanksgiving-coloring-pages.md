Thanksgiving pages are grouped by age on this page. Pick the band that matches how long your child will sit. The drawings are food and harvest. There are no costumes and no movie characters.

[Ages 2–3](#ages-2-3) are one huge object: a [turkey](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/huge-turkey/), a [pie](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/huge-pie/), and an [ear of corn](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/huge-corn/).

[Ages 3–4](#ages-3-4) are the original thick-line set: a [friendly turkey](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/friendly-turkey/), a [pumpkin pie](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/pumpkin-pie/), an [ear of corn](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/ear-of-corn/), a [cornucopia](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/cornucopia/), and a [harvest basket](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/harvest-basket/).

[Ages 4–5](#ages-4-5) are one object with a few parts: a [wheat sheaf](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/wheat-sheaf/), a [cranberry bowl](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/cranberry-bowl/), and [mashed potatoes](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/mashed-potatoes/).

[Ages 5–6](#ages-5-6) are a small scene: a [dinner table](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/dinner-table/), a [turkey with leaves](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/turkey-and-leaves/), and a [harvest porch](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/harvest-porch/).

[Ages 6–8](#ages-6-8) are a fuller scene: a [feast table](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/feast-table/), a [patterned cornucopia](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/patterned-cornucopia/), and a [harvest path](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/harvest-path/).

Leaves and apples are in the [fall coloring pages](/fall-coloring-pages/). December pages are in the [Christmas coloring pages](/christmas-coloring-pages/).

Every picture has a US Letter PDF and an A4 PDF. The [printing guide](/how-to-print/) covers the printer setting.
