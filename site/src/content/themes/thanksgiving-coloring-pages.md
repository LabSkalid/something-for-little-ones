These are grouped by age. Pick the group that matches how long your child will sit. Food and harvest. No costumes, and nothing from a movie.

[Ages 2–3](#ages-2-3) is one huge thing to scribble: a [turkey](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/huge-turkey/), a [pie](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/huge-pie/), or an [ear of corn](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/huge-corn/).

[Ages 3–4](#ages-3-4) still has thick lines: a [friendly turkey](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/friendly-turkey/), a [pumpkin pie](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/pumpkin-pie/), an [ear of corn](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/ear-of-corn/), a [cornucopia](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/cornucopia/), and a [harvest basket](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/harvest-basket/).

[Ages 4–5](#ages-4-5) adds a few parts: a [wheat sheaf](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/wheat-sheaf/), a [cranberry bowl](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/cranberry-bowl/), and [mashed potatoes](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/mashed-potatoes/).

[Ages 5–6](#ages-5-6) is a small scene: a [dinner table](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/dinner-table/), a [turkey with leaves](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/turkey-and-leaves/), and a [harvest porch](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/harvest-porch/).

[Ages 6–8](#ages-6-8) takes a longer afternoon: a [feast table](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/feast-table/), a [patterned cornucopia](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/patterned-cornucopia/), and a [harvest path](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/harvest-path/).

Leaves and apples are in the [fall coloring pages](/fall-coloring-pages/). December is in the [Christmas coloring pages](/christmas-coloring-pages/).
