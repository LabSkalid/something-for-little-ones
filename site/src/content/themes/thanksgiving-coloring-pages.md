faqs:
  - question: How do I pick an age?
    answer: Match the lines, not the birthday. Ages 2–3 are one huge object. Ages 3–4 are thick lines and big spaces. Ages 4–5 add a few parts. Ages 5–6 are a small scene. Ages 6–8 are a fuller scene. The lines stay bold.
  - question: Are these costume pages?
    answer: No. This set is the food and the harvest. A turkey, pie, corn, wheat, and a dinner table. There are no costumes and no movie characters.
  - question: Which page should we print first?
    answer: For a short sitting, use the huge turkey or the ages 3–4 friendly turkey. The pie and the ear of corn are the next step. Save the feast table for a longer afternoon.
  - question: What paper size are the files?
    answer: I made these for letter paper. There's an A4 file too if that's what you have. Same drawing.
