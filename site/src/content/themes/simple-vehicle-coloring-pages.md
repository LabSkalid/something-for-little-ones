These vehicle pages are for about ages 4 to 5. Each one is a single truck, car, boat, or engine with chunky closed shapes. They are a step up from the ages 3–4 car set, and they are not a garden scene.

The set includes a [family car](/simple-vehicle-coloring-pages/family-car/), a [train engine](/simple-vehicle-coloring-pages/train-engine/), a [boat](/simple-vehicle-coloring-pages/little-boat/), a [tractor](/simple-vehicle-coloring-pages/farm-tractor/), and a [fire truck](/simple-vehicle-coloring-pages/fire-truck/). The car and the boat are the shorter pages. The fire truck has a ladder and a hose to finish after the body.

The other ages 4–5 set is the [garden coloring pages](/garden-coloring-pages/). The simpler [car coloring pages](/car-coloring-pages/) are on the ages 3–4 shelf. If this set is finished quickly and they want patterns, try the [ocean scene coloring pages](/ocean-scene-coloring-pages/) for ages 5–6.

There is no street, no shop name, and no number on the engine. Color the body, then the wheels.

Every picture has a US Letter PDF and an A4 PDF. The [printing guide](/how-to-print/) covers the printer setting.
