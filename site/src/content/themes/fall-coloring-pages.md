Fall pages are grouped by age. Pick the group that matches how long your child will sit.

[Ages 2–3](#ages-2-3) is one huge shape: a [leaf](/fall-coloring-pages/huge-leaf/), an [apple](/fall-coloring-pages/huge-apple/), or an [acorn](/fall-coloring-pages/huge-acorn/). Good when they scribble and quit.

[Ages 3–4](#ages-3-4) has thick lines: a [maple leaf](/fall-coloring-pages/maple-leaf/), an [acorn](/fall-coloring-pages/acorn/), a [scarecrow](/fall-coloring-pages/scarecrow/), an [apple basket](/fall-coloring-pages/apple-basket/), and a [squirrel](/fall-coloring-pages/squirrel/).

[Ages 4–5](#ages-4-5) adds a few parts: a [sunflower](/fall-coloring-pages/sunflower/), a [leaf pile](/fall-coloring-pages/leaf-pile/), and a [pumpkin pie](/fall-coloring-pages/pumpkin-pie/).

[Ages 5–6](#ages-5-6) is a small scene: an [apple tree](/fall-coloring-pages/apple-tree/), [porch mums](/fall-coloring-pages/porch-mums/), and a [hay wagon](/fall-coloring-pages/hay-wagon/).

[Ages 6–8](#ages-6-8) is the longer sitting: an [orchard path](/fall-coloring-pages/orchard-path/), an [autumn barn](/fall-coloring-pages/autumn-barn/), and a [forest floor](/fall-coloring-pages/forest-floor/).

This pile is harvest and trees, not costumes. Party pumpkins are in the [Halloween coloring pages](/halloween-coloring-pages/). The late-November dinner is in the [Thanksgiving coloring pages](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/). December is in the [Christmas coloring pages](/christmas-coloring-pages/).
