Christmas pages are grouped by age. None of them use a movie character.

[Ages 2–3](#ages-2-3) is one huge shape: a [tree](/christmas-coloring-pages/huge-tree/), an [ornament](/christmas-coloring-pages/huge-ornament/), or a [present](/christmas-coloring-pages/huge-present/).

[Ages 3–4](#ages-3-4) has thick lines: a [tree](/christmas-coloring-pages/christmas-tree/), [Santa](/christmas-coloring-pages/santa-claus/), a [gingerbread house](/christmas-coloring-pages/gingerbread-house/), a [reindeer](/christmas-coloring-pages/reindeer/), and a [stocking](/christmas-coloring-pages/christmas-stocking/).

[Ages 4–5](#ages-4-5) adds a few parts: a [mitten](/christmas-coloring-pages/striped-mitten/), a [snowman](/christmas-coloring-pages/snowman/), and a [sleigh](/christmas-coloring-pages/gift-sleigh/). More December pictures are in this group too: cocoa, gifts, animals, and little houses.

[Ages 5–6](#ages-5-6) is a small scene: a [fireplace](/christmas-coloring-pages/fireplace/), a [window wreath](/christmas-coloring-pages/window-wreath/), and a [reindeer and tree](/christmas-coloring-pages/reindeer-and-tree/).

[Ages 6–8](#ages-6-8) is the longer sitting: a [living room](/christmas-coloring-pages/living-room/), a [snowy village](/christmas-coloring-pages/snowy-village/), and [winter woods](/christmas-coloring-pages/winter-woods/).

Fall is in the [fall coloring pages](/fall-coloring-pages/). Halloween is in the [Halloween coloring pages](/halloween-coloring-pages/). The late-November dinner is in the [Thanksgiving coloring pages](/thanksgiving-coloring-pages/). The nativity and Bible verses are in the [Christian Christmas coloring pages](/christian-christmas-coloring-pages/).
