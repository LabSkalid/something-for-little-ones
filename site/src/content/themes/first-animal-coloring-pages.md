These animal pages are for about ages 2 to 3. Each drawing is one animal made of a few huge shapes. They are simpler than the animals on the ages 3–4 shelf.

The set is a [puppy](/first-animal-coloring-pages/round-puppy/), a [bird](/first-animal-coloring-pages/simple-bird/), a [butterfly](/first-animal-coloring-pages/simple-butterfly/), a [cat](/first-animal-coloring-pages/sitting-cat/), and a [turtle](/first-animal-coloring-pages/round-turtle/). Print one. The shell and the wings are the shortest jobs.

The other ages 2–3 set is the [big shape coloring pages](/big-shape-coloring-pages/), a ball, an apple, a fish, a cup, and a star. When a child starts filling a shape on purpose and wants smaller parts, move to the [animal coloring pages](/animal-coloring-pages/) for ages 3–4.

Staying inside the line is not the goal yet. If the color goes over the edge, the picture still reads as a puppy or a bird.

Every picture has a US Letter PDF and an A4 PDF. The [printing guide](/how-to-print/) covers the printer setting.
