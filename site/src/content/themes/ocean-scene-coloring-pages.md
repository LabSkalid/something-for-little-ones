These ocean pages are for about ages 5 to 6. If your child can stay with one picture, there's enough here: shells, sails, spots, and waves.

There's a [coral reef](/ocean-scene-coloring-pages/coral-reef/), a [sailboat](/ocean-scene-coloring-pages/sailboat/), a [patterned octopus](/ocean-scene-coloring-pages/patterned-octopus/), a [lighthouse](/ocean-scene-coloring-pages/lighthouse-coast/), and a [sea turtle](/ocean-scene-coloring-pages/sea-turtle/). Start with the sailboat. Save the reef and the octopus for a quiet afternoon.

This is a step up from the [garden coloring pages](/garden-coloring-pages/) for ages 4–5. The other ages 5–6 set is the [dinosaur scene coloring pages](/dinosaur-scene-coloring-pages/). [Town scenes](/town-scene-coloring-pages/) are next, for ages 6–8. If the patterns are too much, the [animal coloring pages](/animal-coloring-pages/) for ages 3–4 are the simple ones.

Pick a repeating part and give it one color, such as every other shell spot or every wave. That's easier than a new color for every tiny shape.
