A coloring page that is too hard ends in a crumpled sheet. One that is too easy is done in thirty seconds. The right page depends less on the number on the birthday cake and more on how much detail your child can handle right now.

Here is a simple way to choose, with a free printable set for each stage. Every child is different, so treat the ages as a starting point and move up or down depending on how your child reacts.

## Ages 2–3: a few huge shapes

At this age many children scribble across the whole page, and that is exactly what coloring is for. Look for pages with one big shape, very thick lines, and nothing small to stay inside.

A page like this is done in a minute or two, and that is fine. Print a few and let your child fill them one after another.

**Try:** [Big Shape coloring pages](/big-shape-coloring-pages/), [First Animal coloring pages](/first-animal-coloring-pages/), [Rocket coloring pages for ages 2–3](/rocket-coloring-pages/#ages-2-3)

## Ages 3–4: thick outlines, a few smaller parts

Children this age start to care about what the picture is. A puppy, a dinosaur, a car, or a princess works well, as long as the outlines are thick and the areas are big, with only a few smaller parts.

**Try:** [Animal coloring pages](/animal-coloring-pages/), [Dinosaur coloring pages](/dinosaur-coloring-pages/), [Car coloring pages](/car-coloring-pages/), [Princess coloring pages](/princess-coloring-pages/)

## Ages 4–5: more parts, still chunky

Now your child can handle a picture with several parts, such as a car with doors, windows, and wheels. Lines are still bold, but there is more to decide, like which part gets which color.

**Try:** [Animal coloring pages for ages 4–5](/animal-coloring-pages/#ages-4-5), [Garden coloring pages](/garden-coloring-pages/), [Simple vehicle coloring pages](/simple-vehicle-coloring-pages/)

## Ages 5–6: busier scenes

Many children at this age enjoy a longer sitting. Scenes with a background, such as an ocean or a dinosaur among plants, give them room to take their time.

**Try:** [Ocean scene coloring pages](/ocean-scene-coloring-pages/), [Dinosaur scene coloring pages](/dinosaur-scene-coloring-pages/)

## Ages 6–8: fuller scenes

Older kids often like pictures with many small parts, such as a town street or a forest scene. The lines should still be bold enough to color comfortably, but the page can take real time.

**Try:** [Town scene coloring pages](/town-scene-coloring-pages/), [Woodland animal coloring pages](/woodland-animal-coloring-pages/)

## How to tell if a page is the right level

- **Too hard:** your child gives up in the first minute, colors over the lines in frustration, or asks you to finish it.
- **Too easy:** the page is done in seconds and your child asks for another one right away, without any interest in the picture.
- **Just right:** your child stays with the page for a few minutes and wants to talk about it.

If a page is almost right, try the next set up or down. Many families keep two levels in the pile, so a younger sibling and an older one can color at the same table.
